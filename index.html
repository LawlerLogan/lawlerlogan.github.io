03

AIAA Design/Build/Fly 2027

Lead Systems Engineer for a 6-ft UAV that ships sensors and deploys, lights up and reels back in a towed sensor in flight. The winch runs on an ESP32-C3 and stepper with a fail-safe ratchet lock; the sensor board is a custom ATtiny85 PCB designed in KiCad.

Mini Arcade Machine

High school senior capstone: a machined wood cabinet with a 7-inch display running a Galaga-style shooter written from scratch in Godot on a Raspberry Pi 5. A Python evdev service turns the arcade buttons into real keystrokes.

Odysseus’s Ship Showdown Carnival Game

Life-size, two-player carnival race station. Hand-shaped conductive pads and buttons, read by two Arduinos, move a miniature ship down a belt-driven slot in each lane and its twin on the TV above. Framed in 2×4s and plywood on casters, with 3D-printed PLA parts.
