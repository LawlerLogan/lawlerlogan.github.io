AIAA Design/Build/Fly 2027

Lead Systems Engineer for a 6-ft UAV that deploys, lights up and reels back in a towed sensor in flight. The winch runs on an ESP32-C3 and stepper with a fail-safe ratchet lock; the sensor board is a custom ATtiny85 PCB designed in KiCad.

04

Mini Arcade Machine

2025 mechatronics capstone: a machined wood cabinet with a 7-inch display and 3 arcade buttons, running a Galaga-style shooter written from scratch in Godot on a Raspberry Pi 5. A Python service polls the buttons every 10 ms and injects real keystrokes through evdev. Presented with a display board and a full engineering notebook.

  • Electronics

05

Odysseus’s Ship Showdown Carnival Game

Life-size, two-player carnival race that runs on a real track and in a matching pixel-art racing game, built in Godot, on the TV above it. Each button press or touch of a hand-shaped conductive pad, read by Arduino, pulls a 3D-printed ship down its belt-driven lane and moves its twin in the game the same distance, so both always show the same race. Framed in 2×4s and plywood on casters.
