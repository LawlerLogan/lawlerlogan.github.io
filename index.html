Odysseus’s Ship Showdown Carnival Game

Life-size, two-player carnival race that runs on a real track and in a matching pixel-art racing game, built in Godot, on the TV above it. Each button press or touch of a hand-shaped conductive pad, read by Arduino, pulls a 3D-printed ship down its belt-driven lane and moves its twin in the game the same distance, so both always show the same race. Framed in 2×4s and plywood on casters.
